Raaga

Singing practice for Carnatic swaras. Two ways to practise, same pitch detection, same saved sessions. Try both and keep the one that suits you.

One note at a time

Raaga Steps

The app asks for each swara in turn and tells you in words how you did: on pitch, a little low, or a different note. Hold it until it turns green, then move on.

  • Live guidance while you sing
  • Tanpura and note guide you can switch off as you improve
  • Recent attempts with frequencies

Listen and repeat

Raaga Echo

Hear the whole phrase, sing it back at your own pace or to a beat, then compare note by note.

  • Your pace: the next swara lights up when yours is heard; or set a tempo in bpm
  • Find a comfortable Sa by choosing or humming
  • A teacher's recording can replace the tone guide
Open Raaga Echo

On an iPhone, open the one you want in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. Recordings stay on your phone. Source and guides: github.com/pdasika82/raaga.
