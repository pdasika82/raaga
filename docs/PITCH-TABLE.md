# Pitches and frequencies, low to high

Reference A4 = 440 Hz (changeable in Settings). One octave doubles the frequency; each
half-step multiplies it by about 1.0595. The app names octaves so that C4 is middle C.

## Svarasthanas from Sa

Offsets are half-steps above Sa; the frequency is Sa's frequency times the ratio. The
equal-tempered ratio is what the app measures against. The just-intonation ratio is the
traditional interval a trained ear aims for; the last column is how far apart they are.

| Half-steps | Carnatic | Hindustani | Equal-tempered ratio | Just ratio | Difference |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | Sa · Shadjam | Sa | 1.0000 | 1/1 = 1.0000 | +0 cents |
| 1 | Ri₁ · Shuddha Rishabham | komal Re | 1.0595 | 16/15 = 1.0667 | +12 cents |
| 2 | Ri₂ · Chatusruti Rishabham | Re | 1.1225 | 9/8 = 1.1250 | +4 cents |
| 3 | Ga₂ · Sadharana Gandharam | komal Ga | 1.1892 | 6/5 = 1.2000 | +16 cents |
| 4 | Ga₃ · Antara Gandharam | Ga | 1.2599 | 5/4 = 1.2500 | -14 cents |
| 5 | Ma₁ · Shuddha Madhyamam | Ma | 1.3348 | 4/3 = 1.3333 | -2 cents |
| 6 | Ma₂ · Prati Madhyamam | tivra Ma | 1.4142 | 45/32 = 1.4062 | -10 cents |
| 7 | Pa · Panchamam | Pa | 1.4983 | 3/2 = 1.5000 | +2 cents |

Shankarabharanam from C3. For another Sa, start from that note's row in the first table
and step down the table by the half-step offsets in the second.
